This public slice is synthetic-only. It records teacher/template/model/runtime identities, deterministic train/eval lineage, leakage checks, per-function metrics, invalid JSON/schema failures, conversion parity, and strict device-receipt identity.

Vendor or talk benchmark numbers are source evidence and must never populate local device-result fields. Real model/service access, terms acceptance, private datasets, mobile quality, physical backend, privacy, energy, and thermal evidence remain separate lanes.
